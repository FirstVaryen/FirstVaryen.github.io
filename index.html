01 — ABOUT ME

Turning messy processes into clear systems

I work where business processes meet data. On the Salesforce side, I take a business from discovery to go-live: I interview the people who do the work, write the requirements and user stories, map how things run today and how they should run, then build it with Flow, Apex and Experience Cloud, test it as every role and deploy it.

On the data side, I clean and analyse data with Python and build dashboards in Power BI, so managers can see what's happening and decide what to do next.

I care about the details users actually feel: plain wording on screen, the right people seeing the right records, and a system that's been tested as the real user, not just the admin.
